A large batch of EMBER (PI H. Akins; Cycle 4) spectra was deployed in late
April — 17 pointings spanning the COSMOS, UDS, and EGS fields, reduced with the
CAMPFIRE pipeline. This was one of several batches in the ongoing EMBER rollout
and does not represent the complete program.

Proprietary program — this update is visible only to users with EMBER access.
