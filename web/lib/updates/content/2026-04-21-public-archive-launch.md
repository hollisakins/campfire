The CAMPFIRE public archive is live. More than 20 JWST/NIRSpec programs were
deployed together, all reduced and inspected with a common pipeline so they can
be browsed and compared side by side. Headline programs include:

- **CAPERS** (M. Dickinson) — 8,172 spectra
- **RUBIES** (de Graaff & Brammer) — 8,522 spectra
- **GTO WIDE** (N. Luetzgendorf) — 5,441 spectra
- **CEERS** (S. Finkelstein) — 1,878 spectra
- **CANUCS** (C. Willott) — 1,756 spectra
- **GO 3073** (M. Castellano) — 1,302 spectra
- **UNCOVER** (Bezanson & Labbe) — 1,015 spectra
- **OCEANS** (R. Simons) — 1,002 spectra

…alongside MoM, EXCELS, GLASS, DIVER, C3PO, SPURS, THRILS, EGS-BUBBLES,
MACS0647-JD, and several DDT programs, spanning the COSMOS, EGS, UDS, and cluster
fields.
