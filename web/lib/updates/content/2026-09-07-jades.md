title: "JADES NIRSpec spectroscopy now available"
date: 2026-09-07
category: data
summary: "The full JADES NIRSpec/MSA archive — 5,297 targets and ~22,000 spectra across GOODS-S and GOODS-N — is now available."
---

The [JADES](/nirspec/metadata/programs/jades) (JWST Advanced Deep Extragalactic
Survey) NIRSpec/MSA archive is now available in CAMPFIRE. This adds 5,297
targets and roughly 22,000 spectra across 26 observations in GOODS-S and
GOODS-N, spanning the Cycle 1 GTO programs (PIDs 1180, 1181, 1210, 1286, 1287)
and the Cycle 2 Origins Field (PID 3215), in PRISM, G140M, G235M, G395M, and
G395H.

Explore the data on the [spectra browser](/nirspec?programs=jades) or the
[program page](/nirspec/metadata/programs/jades), or grab it via the
[Python client](/docs/api/python-client).
